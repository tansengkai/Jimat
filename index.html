Jimat

A private, offline-first budget and savings tracker for Android.

What Jimat does

Jimat (Malay for “save”) is an Android application for recording daily spending, tracking savings goals, and reviewing where the money went. It is built for personal use by a single developer and his household — there is no sign-up, no account on our servers, and no advertising.

  • Record income and expenses with categories, amounts and dates.
  • Track monthly budgets and savings targets.
  • View simple summaries of spending by category and by month.
  • Optionally back up and restore the app's own data files using the user's personal Google Drive.

Your data stays yours

All records are stored locally in the app's private storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly sign in with Google and choose to back up. When you do, the backup goes to your own Google Drive — never to a server operated by Jimat.

Google Drive access

Jimat uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API for one purpose only: to let you save a backup copy of your data to your own Drive, and to read that backup back when you restore. Access is requested with the drive.file scope, which means the app can only see files it created itself — it cannot browse, read or modify any other file in your Drive.

Jimat does not read Gmail, Contacts, Photos, Calendar or any other Google product.
